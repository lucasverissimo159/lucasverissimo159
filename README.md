## 🧭 What I Build

I work mainly at the intersection of **software engineering, infrastructure and automation**.

My projects range from desktop tools for network operations and equipment management to web applications, REST APIs, background job systems and applications that implement algorithms from scratch.

The goal is simple: **turn operational problems into reliable software.**

---

## 🔭 Current Focus

**Scalable Software**  
Designing applications with clear architecture, separation of concerns, reusable components and testable business logic.

**Networks & Infrastructure**  
Building monitoring and management tools around IP networks, UniFi controllers, SSH-based diagnostics and infrastructure operations.

**Data & Automation**  
Creating systems that transform operational data into dashboards, reports, analytics and automated workflows.

**Systems Thinking**  
Applying engineering concepts to software, reliability, observability, automation and problem solving.
